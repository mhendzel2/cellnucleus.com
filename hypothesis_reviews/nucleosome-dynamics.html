Measuring histone incorporation rather than assuming a motion

Deal and colleagues used CATCH-IT in asynchronous Drosophila S2 cells to capture newly made histones and map associated DNA. Their stringent isolation principally retained H3/H4. Active gene bodies and selected regulatory binding sites showed comparatively high incorporation and pulse/chase turnover; Polycomb-associated sites were slower but not inert. This measures replacement-related histone incorporation and loss. It does not directly observe DNA-end breathing, intact-octamer sliding or a specific H2A/H2B exchange mechanism. Deal et al., 2010.

Replication changes histone age and modification composition

Alabert and colleagues combined nascent chromatin capture with isotope labeling and mass spectrometry in cultured human cells. Parental histones carrying modifications were recovered on newly replicated DNA, while new histones initially had different modification profiles. Many new-histone marks approached parental profiles during maturation; H3K9me3 and H3K27me3 instead accumulated progressively on both old and new histones. Thus, new histone deposition and modification recovery are distinguishable processes. Alabert et al., 2015.

Regional modification recovery and linker-histone perturbation

Liu and colleagues followed post-replication H3K27me3 recovery in mouse embryonic stem cells using ChOR-seq. Recovery differed among genomic regions, and depletion of selected H1 variants delayed it. A chronic H1c/d/e knockout and an inducible H1c/e comparison supplied complementary evidence. These results support an H1 contribution in this system, while remaining H1 variants and broader perturbation effects limit a claim that every change is caused solely by physical compaction. Liu et al., 2023.

What remains distinct in a nucleosome-dynamics argument

The three studies provide substantive evidence for dynamic histone incorporation and modification maturation in their examined systems. They do not jointly establish a universal turnover half-life, one-base-pair positional precision in every cell, or a fixed assignment of all remodeling functions to enzyme families. Nor does persistent total modification abundance mean that individual histones remain stationary. Those are different assertions requiring different evidence. Here, “dynamics” refers to the measured incorporation, loss and recovery processes above. Deal et al., 2010; Alabert et al., 2015; Liu et al., 2023.

Replication-coupled handling can affect inherited silencing — 12 October 2026

Charlton and colleagues connected parental histone handling to inheritance of a measured silencing state in fission yeast. Mrc1 connector-domain and Mcm2 histone-binding mutants disrupted lagging-strand recycling and produced asymmetric loss of H3K9me-associated reporter silencing in daughter lineages. Their biochemical and genetic comparisons support a recycling mechanism in that organism. This supplies a cellular inheritance readout beyond total mark recovery; it does not establish human transgenerational inheritance or a requirement for a liquid chromatin compartment. Charlton et al. (2024).

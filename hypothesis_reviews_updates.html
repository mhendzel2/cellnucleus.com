What counts as a significant advance?

  • A direct causal perturbation, a strong orthogonal replication, or a result that materially changes a review's scope.
  • A methodological advance that resolves a central inferential limitation rather than merely adding a new visualization.
  • A source whose experimental system, assay, and limitations can be stated clearly.

What does not happen automatically?

  • A search hit never edits a verdict, evidence score, or review text on its own.
  • Preprints are labelled as such and do not alone trigger a verdict revision.
  • Abstract language is not treated as proof of a mechanism or general rule.

How to read an update

Each published record states the study type, why it matters, a limitation or interpretation boundary, and the explicit effect on the associated review.

2026-09-16 Primary research

Cohesin reshapes replication fork contacts to aid fork slowing and reversal

Why it was added: In engineered human HCT116 cell lines under replication stress, acute cohesin or NIPBL perturbation, separation-of-function cohesin mutants, DNA-fibre assays, electron microscopy, and Repli-C linked loop-extruding cohesin to inter-replicon contacts, controlled fork slowing, and fork reversal.

Interpretation boundary: The work tests chemically induced replication stress in cultured human cell lines. It extends a context-specific function of loop-extruding cohesin; it does not independently establish the general TAD or loop-extrusion model, or a clinical benefit.

Effect on this review: No change to the Strong Evidence verdict. The study adds a functionally specific replication-stress consequence of loop-extruding cohesin. Read primary article

2026-09-07 Primary research

Loss of SUMOylation drives aberrant PRC1 clustering and 3D genome rewiring independent of H3K27me3

Why it was added: In Drosophila wing imaginal discs, SUMO RNAi produced larger, less dynamic PRC1 foci and rewired contacts between Polycomb and active TADs without a global loss of H3K27me3; the contact changes coincided with gene misregulation.

Interpretation boundary: This tissue-specific RNAi perturbation did not test transmission through S phase or mitosis, and the topological and transcriptional changes do not by themselves prove that altered PRC1 condensation is the sole causal bridge.

Effect on this review: No change to Enzymatic Propagation Better Supported. It clarifies one way PRC1 material properties can alter architecture without showing that condensate persistence carries epigenetic memory. Read primary article

2026-08-24 Primary research

Chromatin accessibility classification of TAD boundaries discloses new architectural proteins

Why it was added: In K562 cells, chromatin-accessibility-resolved Hi-C and protein-occupancy analyses identified CTCF/cohesin/REST and ZNF316/EMSY boundary modules. Editing the ZNF316/EMSY binding region at one robust CTCF-free boundary increased contacts across that boundary and was associated with altered expression of nearby genes.

Interpretation boundary: This is a single human leukemia cell line and a selected CTCF-free boundary. It supports context-dependent insulation mechanisms, not a claim that all TADs or all boundaries have the same regulatory effect.

Effect on this review: No change to the current verdict. It supports a causal, locus-specific structural contribution while leaving generality and causal direction unresolved. Read primary article

2026-08-21 Primary research

RNA decay rate as a control point for long non-coding RNA function: a BRIC-based causal analysis of NEAT1

Why it was added: In HeLa cells, inhibitor-free BRIC half-life measurements plus bidirectional exosome perturbations changed NEAT1 abundance, which scaled paraspeckle number and size without higher paraspeckle-protein mRNA. The study also reported selected gene-expression changes.

Interpretation boundary: This is a HeLa-cell study with selected targets; it evaluates assembly control rather than physiological roles across tissues or whether paraspeckles are broadly functional under normal conditions.

Effect on this review: No change to Structure Established, Function Uncertain. The work strengthens an assembly-regulation mechanism but does not establish broad physiological function. Read PubMed record

Nuclear Metabolism: Acetyl-CoA and Local ATP Evidence

This HTML presents a selective primary-evidence overview. Every retained scientific paragraph and reference was appraised at the access depth listed below. Detailed Methods, raw data, exhaustive literature coverage and the associated source document remain outside the checked scope.

Cellular carbon supply and histone acetylation

Wellen and colleagues linked ATP-citrate lyase, which produces acetyl-CoA from citrate, with histone acetylation in mammalian cells. Growth-factor and differentiation-associated increases, and responses to glucose availability, depended on this enzyme in their experimental models. [wellen2009]

The findings connect cellular metabolism with a chromatin modification. Cellular enzyme dependence does not by itself establish a separate, autonomous nuclear metabolic circuit or control of every histone mark. [wellen2009]

Evidence for acetyl-CoA generation in nuclei

Sutendra and colleagues reported functional pyruvate-dehydrogenase-complex subunits in mammalian nuclei. In isolated functional nuclei, reduced nuclear complex activity was associated with less newly synthesized acetyl-CoA and core-histone acetylation. [sutendra2014]

Reciprocal changes in nuclear and mitochondrial complex abundance led the authors to suggest redistribution between compartments. Those abundance observations are distinct from directly tracking a protein's movement, and fraction-purity controls were not inspected at Abstract depth. [sutendra2014]

A stimulus-associated nuclear ATP route

Wright and colleagues reported ADP-ribose-derived nuclear ATP generation involving NUDIX5 in progestin-responsive breast cancer cells. Their Abstract links this route with hormone-associated chromatin remodeling, transcription and proliferation. [wright2016]

This is evidence for a particular stimulus and cell context. It does not establish the dominant ATP source in every nucleus, a universal metabolic rate or a treatment recommendation. [wright2016]

Interpreting local metabolic evidence

The studies distinguish cellular substrate supply, nuclear acetyl-CoA production and stimulus-associated ATP generation. They support specific links with chromatin while leaving whole-cell exchange, compartment purity and model dependence central to interpretation. [wellen2009] [sutendra2014] [wright2016]

Primary Abstracts and bibliographic identities were inspected. Detailed localization controls, all flux measurements, raw data and quantitative tissue-wide generalizations were not independently appraised. [wellen2009] [sutendra2014] [wright2016]

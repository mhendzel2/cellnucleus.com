The initiating modification and its catalytic specificity

PARP1 and PARP2 use NAD+ for protein ADP-ribosylation, which can involve a single ADP-ribose unit or a polymeric PAR signal. Suskiewicz and colleagues found that HPF1 and the PARP catalytic domain form a composite active site supporting serine-linked modification. [suskiewicz2020]

The primary study combined a HPF1-PARP2 co-structure, PARP1 NMR, biochemical modification assays and human 293T-cell complementation. The agreement among these approaches supports catalytic specificity; truncated protein structures alone would not establish cellular regulation. [suskiewicz2020]

Initial attachment to a protein and extension of a PAR chain are distinguishable biochemical events. The study describes HPF1-dependent serine modification together with reduced PARP auto-modification in its assays; it does not establish a universal PARP1-versus-PARP2 division of chain length or branching in every cell. [suskiewicz2020]

PAR-associated recruitment has a time course

Altmeyer and colleagues observed rapid, transient PAR-dependent accumulation of FUS, EWS and TAF15 at DNA-damaged regions in human U-2-OS cells. Endogenous-protein observations complemented tagged-protein imaging, and positively charged RGG-rich regions contributed to recruitment. [altmeyer2015]

In the same study, depletion of the PAR-degrading enzyme PARG prolonged the local light-diffraction response. This connects signal turnover with the duration of the associated assembly response; light diffraction remains an indirect physical readout, rather than a direct assay of repaired DNA. [altmeyer2015]

An endogenous replication-associated setting

Hanzlikova and colleagues detected PAR at replication sites during normal S phase, reported dependence on lagging-strand fragment processing, and observed XRCC1 recruitment. They interpreted the findings as PARP1 surveillance of unligated Okazaki fragments. [hanzlikova2018]

This adds a replication-associated context to induced-damage studies. It does not establish that a uniform PAR burst, concentration or lifetime applies to every nucleus, and PAR detection alone does not resolve the full molecular composition of an assembly. [hanzlikova2018] [altmeyer2015]

Evidence boundaries

Primary Abstracts and citation identities were inspected for all three references. Descriptive Results and selected figure legends were also inspected for the HPF1 and FET-protein studies. Detailed Methods, all quantitative controls and a complete catalogue of PAR readers were not appraised. [suskiewicz2020] [altmeyer2015] [hanzlikova2018]

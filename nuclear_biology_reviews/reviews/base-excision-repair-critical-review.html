Repair chemistry demonstrated in a defined system

Kubota and colleagues reconstituted repair of a uracil:guanine mismatch with purified human uracil-DNA glycosylase, AP endonuclease, DNA polymerase beta and DNA ligase III. The experiment connects base removal, backbone processing, gap filling and ligation for this particular substrate. [kubota1996]

XRCC1 was not absolutely required in that reconstituted reaction, but it limited polymerase-beta strand displacement and improved ligation after a single-nucleotide repair patch. Interaction assays and an XRCC1-dependent shift of a polymerase-DNA complex supported a coordinating scaffold role. [kubota1996]

These observations establish a workable biochemical route. They do not establish that every nuclear base lesion follows this exact protein combination, that DNA ligase III is universally obligatory, or that a purified reaction reproduces chromatin repair. [kubota1996]

BER intermediates also engage damage sensing

Demin and colleagues found excessive PARP1 engagement at BER intermediates in XRCC1-deficient cells, including fibroblasts from XRCC1-mutated disease. Their primary Abstract reports that XRCC1-associated polymerase-beta and ligase-III complexes limited this engagement. [demin2021]

In those deficient models, persistent PARP1 engagement made repair intermediates less accessible to polymerase beta and impeded repair; PARP1 loss rescued the reported BER and base-damage-resistance defects. This makes coordination of intermediate access part of the biological interpretation of XRCC1 function. [demin2021]

The deficient-cell result does not mean that PARP1 is generally harmful or unnecessary. Together, the two studies support a distinction between a chemically sufficient repair reaction and regulation of the protein occupancy of its intermediates. [kubota1996] [demin2021]

Evidence limits

Both primary Abstracts and citation identities were inspected. The purified-protein study identifies biochemical steps; the deficient-cell study identifies a context-dependent coordination defect. Detailed assay controls and quantitative cellular kinetics were not reanalysed, and claims about long-patch repair, all glycosylases or clinical outcomes are not made here. [kubota1996] [demin2021]

Local chromatin structure

ChromEMT in chemically fixed human small-airway epithelial and mitotic U2OS cells showed heterogeneous chromatin chains and packing, rather than a regular 30-nm folding hierarchy. Native cryo-ET in interphase CEM T-lymphoblasts instead resolved relaxed, variable local nucleosome arrangements near the nuclear envelope. These methods measure different aspects of structure and use different preparations. [1] [2]

Kreysing et al. found an elongated, heterogeneous arrangement approximately 37 nm wide at the nuclear periphery of primary resting human CD4+ T cells. The organization lacked canonical ordered 30-nm fiber architecture. Linker connectivity was predicted from cryo-ET nucleosome positions and checked against density; this does not map every DNA connection or active chromatin region. [3]

Contact domains and compartments

Rao et al. used in situ Hi-C to map population contact frequencies in GM12878 and other cell lines. Regions with enhanced internal contact frequencies formed domains, while long-range contact patterns separated compartments with different chromatin-mark and accessibility associations. The authors observed preferentially convergent CTCF motifs at many loop anchors. A contact map does not specify a unique structure present in every cell, and correlation with gene activity does not establish a causal regulatory mechanism. [4]

What single-cell tracing adds

Bintu et al. traced selected human chromosome 21 regions by multiplexed DNA-FISH. TAD-like globular domains appeared in individual cells, but their boundaries varied between cells and preferentially occurred near CTCF/cohesin sites. In RAD21-depleted HCT116 cells, population domain patterns and preferred boundary positions were weakened or lost while single-cell domains persisted. These fixed-cell observations distinguish ensemble boundaries from the existence of local compaction; they do not show live folding trajectories. [5]

A locus-specific live measurement

Gabriele et al. labeled the boundaries of the Fbn2 TAD in mouse embryonic stem cells. Live trajectories, perturbations and Bayesian inference indicated that the fully bridged loop was rare and transient, with an estimated median lifetime of approximately 10–30 minutes. This was one silent locus in one cell type; neither the lifetime nor the inferred fraction can be assigned to every chromatin loop or TAD. Fully looped, partially extruded and unlooped states are distinct. [6]

How to combine the evidence

Local nucleosome arrangements, ensemble contact domains, single-cell spatial domains and time-resolved loops are different observables. Together they support heterogeneous chromatin organization across the sampled scales. They do not establish a universal stable fiber, a single fixed domain conformation or one mechanism sufficient to explain all genome organization. Each inference should retain the cell type, region, preparation and measurement that produced it. [1] [2] [3] [4] [5] [6]

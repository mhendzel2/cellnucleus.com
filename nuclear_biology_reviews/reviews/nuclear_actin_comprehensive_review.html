Actin in transcription-initiation evidence

Hofmann and colleagues detected beta-actin at two inducible promoters and in RNA-polymerase-II-associated preparations. Antibody inhibition and purified nuclear-component assays supported a contribution to transcription initiation and pre-initiation-complex formation. [hofmann2004]

This evidence concerns selected promoters and initiation systems. It does not establish that all nuclear actin must be filamentous, that every gene has identical dependence or that all RNA polymerases use the same mechanism. [hofmann2004]

Filaments observed during a damage response

Belin and colleagues observed damage-associated nuclear actin filaments in HeLa cells using live-cell probes and phalloidin in cells without the expressed probe. The structures included nucleoplasmic and nucleolus-associated patterns. [belin2015]

Formin-2 and combined Spire-1/Spire-2 dependence supported regulated assembly in the tested setting. Nuclear-actin and Formin-2-deficient models accumulated more 53BP1 or phosphorylated-H2AX damage foci. [belin2015]

The study reported probe-dependent visibility and filament stabilization, as well as limited overlap between filaments and damage foci. Foci are damage-signaling endpoints; these observations do not show a filament at every break or directly certify completed backbone repair. [belin2015]

Interpretation and corrected identity

Transcription-associated actin and damage-associated filaments are distinct observations. Together they support context-dependent nuclear functions, without establishing one universal actin structure for transcription and repair. [hofmann2004] [belin2015]

The primary correction replaces the title typography Spire-½ with Spire-1/2. It does not correct experimental results. The corrected article title is used in the bibliography. [belin2015correction]

The transcription study was inspected at primary Abstract depth; selected descriptive Results were inspected for the filament study, and the complete correction notice was read. Detailed Methods, all controls and disease extrapolations remain outside the checked scope. [hofmann2004] [belin2015] [belin2015correction]

Replication can generate a repair-signaling substrate

Hanzlikova and colleagues detected endogenous poly(ADP-ribose), or PAR, at replication sites in normal S-phase cells. Their Abstract reports dependence on lagging-strand replication and increased PAR when Okazaki-fragment processing was impaired. [hanzlikova2018]

PARP activation in this setting recruited XRCC1, and human cells deficient in PARP activity or XRCC1 were more sensitive to impaired fragment processing. The authors interpreted these findings as surveillance and repair of unligated Okazaki fragments. [hanzlikova2018]

The study connects replication with single-strand-break repair machinery. Its detected signal and perturbation dependence do not establish a universal lesion count or imply that every endogenous PAR signal originates from DNA replication. [hanzlikova2018]

DNA-end processing shapes pathway competition

Bunting and colleagues reported that 53BP1 loss in Brca1-deficient cell models promoted ATM-dependent DNA-end processing and restored homologous-recombination repair. Lig4 deficiency reduced aberrant chromosome joining without rescuing the homologous-recombination defect. [bunting2010]

The contrasting outcomes separate access to recombination-compatible DNA ends from the joining of unrepaired breaks. They support competition between repair routes in these deficient models, without assigning one fixed pathway to every lesion or normal cell. [bunting2010]

Assembly and signaling are additional layers

Kilic and colleagues observed dynamic 53BP1-marked repair compartments with fusion and fission in cultured human cells. Their primary Abstract distinguishes the sensitivity of 53BP1 assembly from upstream gamma-H2AX and MDC1 accumulation. [kilic2019]

Artificial 53BP1 optoDroplets enriched p53, and disruption-associated conditions reduced the reported p53 response. These observations link compartment behavior with signaling in the tested systems, but do not demonstrate that liquid-liquid phase separation is the sole assembly mechanism or required for every completed repair event. [kilic2019]

Reading the network evidence

Replication-associated PAR, DNA-end processing and 53BP1 organization are experimentally distinct observations. Calling them a network is a synthesis of their relationships, not a demonstration that all repair pathways share the same mechanism. The Abstracts were inspected; selected primary figure captions were also available for the 53BP1 study. [hanzlikova2018] [bunting2010] [kilic2019]

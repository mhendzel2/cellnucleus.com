Checked scientific scope

Detailed Methods, raw data, an exhaustive literature search and the associated source document are outside this review.

A focus can mark a damage-associated chromatin response

Rogakou and colleagues used an antibody to gamma-H2AX, the serine-139-phosphorylated form of H2AX, to observe nuclear foci after irradiation and at localized laser-damaged nuclear regions. Their Abstract also describes band-like signals on irradiated mitotic chromosome arms. [rogakou1999]

These observations associate the H2AX modification with damaged chromosomal regions. Although focus numbers were comparable to induced break numbers in their experimental setting, that relationship is not an exact conversion factor for every specimen, damage type or imaging condition. [rogakou1999]

A chromatin signal is not direct evidence that a DNA backbone has been restored. Focus appearance, intensity or disappearance therefore needs to be interpreted alongside the endpoint being measured, rather than treated as a stand-alone proof of completed repair. [rogakou1999]

53BP1 compartments have distinct dynamics

Kilic and colleagues described droplet-like fusion and fission of 53BP1-marked compartments in human cultured cells. Endogenous tagging and live microscopy were part of the evidence summarized in the primary Abstract. [kilic2019]

The reported sensitivity of 53BP1 assembly differed from that of gamma-H2AX and MDC1. Artificial optoDroplet experiments provided further evidence for 53BP1 self-assembly and p53-associated signaling, but do not make all focus components equivalent or uniquely establish an endogenous phase boundary. [kilic2019]

Protein function and focus appearance answer different questions

Bunting and colleagues linked 53BP1 to DNA-end processing: its loss restored recombination in Brca1-deficient cell models, while Lig4 loss mainly prevented aberrant joining. This is genetic evidence about pathway competition, rather than a measurement of the material state of a visible focus. [bunting2010]

Together, the studies support separating a damage-associated chromatin mark, compartment dynamics and a repair-pathway endpoint. A bright or liquid-like focus does not by itself specify which DNA ends are repaired, by which route, or with what fidelity. [rogakou1999] [kilic2019] [bunting2010]

Access and specimen limits

Primary Abstracts and bibliographic identities were inspected; selected primary figure captions were additionally available for Kilic et al. These are experimental cell observations, not a calibration of all endogenous foci in living tissues. Detailed Methods, full supplementary material and raw-data reanalysis remain outside the checked scope. [rogakou1999] [kilic2019] [bunting2010]

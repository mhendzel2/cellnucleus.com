Elemental contrast and molecular identification

Electron spectroscopic imaging selects electron-energy-loss signals to map elements. Ahmed and colleagues used phosphorus and nitrogen maps, together with shape and dimensions, to interpret nuclear chromatin and ribonucleoprotein-rich regions. Phosphorus can occur in both DNA and RNA, so a bright map or a display color is insufficient to identify a unique molecule, histone variant, gene or transcript. Their lineage-marker fluorescence supplies separate cell-identity evidence rather than making elemental contrast sequence-specific. Ahmed et al., 2010.

A correlative mouse-embryo experiment

The study correlated lineage-marker immunofluorescence with ESI in fixed, embedded 70 nm sections. Blastocyst epiblast had more dispersed chromatin than trophectoderm and primitive-endoderm cells; later epiblast had larger compact regions. Image-cluster analysis sampled 20–30 fields from serial sections of approximately 10 cells per cell type. Those fields describe sampled material and must not be counted as20–30 independent embryos or complete three-dimensional nuclei. Ahmed et al., 2010.

Phosphorus tomography is a separate reconstruction

Aronova and colleagues developed QuEST to reconstruct phosphorus distributions from elemental tilt series. Their abstract and figure legends describe embedded Drosophila larval-cell sections, nuclear chromatin and ribosomal-region measurements, with simulations and background controls. These are three-dimensional elemental reconstructions of a section volume, not live imaging or a complete sequence-resolved genome map. The quoted 2.7 nm voxel dimension and phosphorus sensitivity are experiment-specific quantities; voxel spacing by itself does not establish achieved spatial resolution. Aronova et al., 2007.

Quantification and biological inference need separate checks

The QuEST legends explicitly show substantial beam-associated shrinkage of embedding material and evaluate reconstruction noise. Ahmed’s correlative experiment likewise relies on prepared sections and image classification. A comparison should therefore distinguish specimen geometry, elemental quantification and biological interpretation. Oct4-null epiblast compaction is evidence of an association under a regulatory perturbation, not proof that compacting DNA alone removes pluripotency. RNP-like morphology is not a measured RNA-synthesis rate. Aronova et al., 2007; Ahmed et al., 2010.
